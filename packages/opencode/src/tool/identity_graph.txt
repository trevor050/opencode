Write the K-12 identity graph for an authorized operation.

Use this to connect people, accounts, groups, roles, applications, data systems, vendors, and devices. Edges should represent relationships like has_role, member_of, administers, owns_data, likely_access, validates_against, or vendor_integrates_with. Mark low-confidence public inferences as low confidence and validate them later with authorized exports or authenticated review.

Keep people nodes professional and engagement-relevant. Do not store private-life details, home addresses, family information, unrelated personal social media, or anything unrelated to authorized account/workflow risk.
